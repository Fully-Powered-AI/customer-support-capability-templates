# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. The approach draws on two Apache-2.0 packages in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins: its ticket triage method (category, priority, duplicate check, route) and its customer escalation method (when a ticket leaves ordinary support and what the receiving person needs). The invented rule set in the worked example is modelled on the same triage source: its category list, its four priority levels and their first-reply targets; the example's threshold of three tickets for a pattern follows the escalation source. Working from an export against the team's own written rules, the reason on every line, counting the reply clock in working hours and the stop for a person's decision are Fully Powered AI's own. No third-party text is reused.
