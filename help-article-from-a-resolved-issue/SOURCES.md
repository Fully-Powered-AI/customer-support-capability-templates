The shape of the work (work out the problem, the fix and who it affects; choose among how-to, troubleshooting, question-and-answer and known-issue articles; write titles and search terms in the customer's language with exact error text; check existing articles to decide between updating and writing new; hand over with publishing notes, a reviewer and a review date; a colleague new to the issue tries the steps before publishing) draws on the knowledge-base article method in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0). Fully Powered AI added the split between what a customer can do and what only staff can do, the removal of customer details with a record of each kind removed, the trace from every statement and step to the ticket, the flag for steps the record does not confirm, the stop conditions, and the rule that the knowledge-base owner approves and publishes.
