The reading of a promise draws on one method in CALLE-AI/awesome-phone-call-agents (MIT), its call-agent commitment tracker: telling a promise apart by whether it carries a deadline, carries none or waits on the other party; counting only the agent's own words as the organization's promise and never what the customer says they will do; treating a promise found on a call as something to check, not as proof it was broken; and keeping sensitive details out of the quoted evidence. The fixed rules for reading deadlines, the flags, the matching against follow-up records, the defaults, the questions for the team leader and the invented example are Fully Powered AI's own. No third-party text is reused.
