From it: a separate limit on inactivity for each kind of ticket status, with the example's default limits picked from the ranges it suggests and a shorter limit for tickets nobody owns; counting replies, notes and status changes as activity while automatic updates do not count, and falling back to status changes alone, said openly, when nothing better is available; stating which limits were used; three ways to resolve a ticket left with the customer (another reminder, handing it back to a named owner in the team, or closing it once a person agrees); spotting likely duplicates from one customer raised close together and described alike, and proposing a link rather than merging; judging each person's queue by more than how many tickets it holds; putting the longest overdue tickets first; and proposing changes while making none. Fully Powered AI's own: working from an export with set column headings and stopping on any heading that does not match; counting working days around the team's closed days; the team's reminder and closing rules that point each waiting ticket to one of the three ways; the separate list of tickets whose customer has answered while the status still shows them waiting; weighing load as size points times an age factor, with heavy and light marks against the team average and the arithmetic shown; comparing with the previous week's sheet; and accounting for every open ticket exactly once.
