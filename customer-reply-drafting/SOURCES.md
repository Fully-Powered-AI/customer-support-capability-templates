# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. The shape of the work (read the situation, choose the tone, build the reply in four parts, add notes for the sender, check before handing over) and the writing defaults used when your tone guide is silent draw on the response-drafting method in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0). Fully Powered AI added the rule that your tone guide and approved answers are the only authority, the point-by-point record of what the customer raised with a result for each point, and the list of promises with the rule that allows each. No third-party text is reused.

- **Fully Powered AI:** Fully Powered AI's own rules for prepared work: approved material as the only authority, every promise traced to the rule that allows it, and a person who sends.
- **[anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins)** at commit `02ffa99ff00ce4d3046ccebabb8357cb39f3ab06`, `customer-support/skills/draft-response`, Apache-2.0. The method this template adapts: establish the situation, channel and relationship first; fit the tone to the situation; build a reply that acknowledges, answers, gives next steps and closes; size it to the channel; hand it over with private notes for the sender; and name the situations that go to someone senior before a reply. The writing defaults used when a tone guide is silent also follow its guidance: first person for the sender's own commitments, the customer's own terms, specific dates, no passing of blame, and shorter replies for chat than for email. No text is reused. Its licence notice at that commit is in [notices/anthropics-knowledge-work-plugins-LICENSE.txt](notices/anthropics-knowledge-work-plugins-LICENSE.txt).
