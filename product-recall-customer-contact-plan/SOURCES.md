# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. The way of working draws on one published method, under the MIT licence: the recall phoning method in CALLE-AI/awesome-phone-call-agents, which was built for automated calls. From it come these ideas: passing on only the wording the business signed off and the return routes it really operates; answering only from a list of cleared answers and handing every other question to a person; giving no view on the hazard, with comfort counted as a view too; confirming who is on the line before saying anything about the purchase, and keeping voicemails free of product detail; taking no payment details; recording what became of the item, whether kept, handed on or thrown out; telling a missed call apart from a call that needs a person; and, at its centre, treating a good conversation as different from a finished recall, so a customer is finished only once a named person confirms the item came back, with each measure reported against its own base. Fully Powered AI turned it into a plan a care team works by hand: the affected list built from the order export with default column headers and a stop when a header does not match, unclear rows set aside for the notice's approver rather than ruled on, held customers kept on the list with their reason, an email or letter draft and a phone script read by a person, a tracker built from the team's own contact log and the warehouse's returns log with units counted for each customer, and the next contacts due. No third-party text is reused.

- **Fully Powered AI:** Fully Powered AI's own rules for prepared work: the business's own approved record as the only authority, every unclear case written as a question for a named role, and a person who approves, contacts and records.
- **[CALLE-AI/awesome-phone-call-agents](https://github.com/CALLE-AI/awesome-phone-call-agents)** at commit `14ab8d21081ead9063a6af5f86804bc9cdafb626`, `skills/recall-outreach/SKILL.md`, MIT. The recall phoning method this template adapts from automated calls to a contact plan worked by people: relay only signed-off wording and the remedies the business runs; answer only from cleared answers and pass the rest to a person; no hazard advice or comfort; confirm the customer before naming the purchase; nothing about the product on a voicemail; no payment details; record what became of the item; separate a plain no-answer from a call needing a person; and never count a contact as a finished recall, with each measure on its own base. The boundary notes and worked outcomes behind these ideas are in the same package, references/safety.md and references/examples.md, same repository, commit and licence. No text is reused.
