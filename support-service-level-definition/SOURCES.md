# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Three openly licensed methods shaped it. From the support service level method in LeadMagic/gtm-skills (MIT): a first-response target and a resolution target for each priority level; counting the first reply only when a person writes it; setting each target at a pace the team can keep up week after week; keeping the top level narrow; letting the highest level run on a longer watch than the rest; a share of tickets each target is meant to be met for; and raising an open ticket to a lead part-way through its target. From the capacity planning method in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0): counting only the hours people truly have once meetings, other work and leave are taken out, and never planning those hours to their limit. From the operations capacity method in alirezarezvani/claude-skills (MIT): planning against a busy week rather than the average one, a ceiling of about 80% on the load of queued work, and agreeing in advance what happens in the busiest weeks. Fully Powered AI's own: working every target out from the team's export by a fixed position rule and a ladder of steps, the minimum count below which no target is proposed, testing wanted targets against the record, the stop on any export column it cannot match, the list of tickets left out and the note when they could flatter a figure, estimating the work from the lead's own minutes per ticket, checking each week ahead against the roster, the numbered questions for the lead, and keeping the targets internal with no customer-facing terms. No third-party text is reused.
