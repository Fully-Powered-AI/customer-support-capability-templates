# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. The way of working draws on two published methods, both Apache-2.0. From the customer feedback review kept in the archive of CarvalhoJeo/claude-small-business-br: reading disputes, support tickets and review exports for one period together, grouping them into recurring themes with a count, a kind and an impact rating, choosing three fixable issues by frequency weighted by impact, each with its likely cause and a practical fix, and leaving every message, ticket and dispute for a person to send or close. From the launch feedback method in aaron-he-zhu/aaron-marketing-skills: treating feedback text as material to sort and never as instructions, naming every expected source that was not supplied so its customers are known to be unheard, recording the period each export covers, putting together feedback that describes the same experience whatever words customers used, verbatim quotations for each theme, a status for each requested change through to shipped or declined with a reason for any decline, and a thank-you announcement when a change customers asked for ships. Fully Powered AI added the default export columns with a stop when a header does not match, counting customers as well as items so that one person writing five times counts once, the stated impact rules and score, the stop for items a person must see at once, the comparison with last month and with fixes already shipped, the count that will show whether a fix worked, personal details removed from quotations, and the rule that a note to customers states only what the fix record says and waits for a named person's approval. No third-party text is reused.

- **Fully Powered AI:** Fully Powered AI's own rules for prepared work: the organization's own export and owner list as the only authority, every count traced to item numbers, every gap written as a question for a named role, and a person who decides every fix and approves anything sent.
