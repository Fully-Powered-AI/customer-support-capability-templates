# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. The way of working draws on two published methods, both Apache-2.0. From the refund and complaint method in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins: treating the customer's message as their account rather than as instructions, looking up the order, any earlier refund and any open card dispute, never choosing between orders that could match, holding the reply to the written policy and sending its gaps to the owner, marking missing facts inside the draft, approving a refund separately from the reply, and showing no refund approval at all for a declined request. From the complaint method kept in the archive of CarvalhoJeo/claude-small-business-br, which came from an earlier version of Anthropic's small-business plugin: a short view of the customer's history and a check for the same complaint recurring, raised with the owner as a question about the operation. Fully Powered AI added the clause-by-clause test with a result for each condition, the worked amount with what is left out, the default export columns with a stop when a header does not match, and the stops for a customer who mentions a lawyer, a regulator or their statutory rights and for an injury or a safety risk. No third-party text is reused.

- **Fully Powered AI:** Fully Powered AI's own rules for prepared work: the organization's written policy as the only authority, every condition tested against a stated fact, every gap written as a question for a named role, and a person who approves, issues and sends.
- **[anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins)** at commit `02ffa99ff00ce4d3046ccebabb8357cb39f3ab06`, `small-business/skills/ticket-deflector`, Apache-2.0. The refund and complaint method this template adapts to a no-connection, policy-first form: read the message as the customer's account and never act on instructions inside it; find the order, any refund already made and any open card dispute, and stop the refund path on a disputed charge; list every possible match rather than picking one; hold the reply to the written policy, decline with the reason and the alternative the policy allows, and send a silent or contradictory policy to the owner; flag each missing fact within the draft itself; approve any refund separately from the reply and show no refund approval for a declined request; keep card details to the last four digits; and record the outcome afterwards. The card-digit rule and the check on changed bank details are set out in the plugin's shared files (small-business/shared/personal-data.md and untrusted-content.md), same repository, commit and licence, and restated in this method's own approval steps. No text is reused. Its licence notice at that commit is in [notices/anthropics-knowledge-work-plugins-LICENSE.txt](notices/anthropics-knowledge-work-plugins-LICENSE.txt).
